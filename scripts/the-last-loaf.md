# "She Gave Her Last Bread to a Stranger. Then He Said Her Name."
Target length: 55-60 s (vertical 9:16). Cast: NARRATOR (warm, close, low), MARIA (30s, tired, gentle), THE STRANGER (older man, soft, weary). Boy Daniel has no lines (asleep).
Performance rule: real = restraint. Speak, do not announce. Let silence do the work. Take breaths before big lines.

## Voice script with direction (ElevenLabs v3 audio tags in brackets; remove tags for older models)
[SOUND: heavy rain, distant thunder, low warm drone. Under it: a clock ticking.]

NARRATOR [low, intimate, almost a whisper]: One loaf. One knock. And a choice.
[beat 1.0 s - rain only]
NARRATOR [soft, steady]: Maria had not eaten since morning. Her little boy, Daniel, slept on the floor beside her, his stomach empty.
[SOUND: three slow knocks. Silence. Rain.]
MARIA [whispering, afraid]: Who would be out in a storm like this?
[SOUND: door hinge, rain louder.]
STRANGER [weak, hoarse, hood dripping]: Please... anything. Just a little bread.
[beat 1.2 s - rain, her breath]
NARRATOR [slower]: She looked at the loaf. She looked at her sleeping son.
[beat 0.8 s]
MARIA [voice trembling, then steady]: ...Come in. Out of the rain.
NARRATOR [tender]: She broke the bread in two. Half for the stranger. Half for her boy.
[SOUND: bread tearing, soft strings enter.]
[beat 1.0 s - he eats slowly, a sound of a held-back sob]
STRANGER [barely a breath]: Thank you, Maria.
[beat 0.6 s - music drops out]
MARIA [stunned, almost silent]: ...I never told you my name.
[beat 1.5 s - only rain, then rain fades]
NARRATOR [quiet]: By morning, he was gone.
[SOUND: birds, warm light, strings rise.]
NARRATOR [warm, wonder]: But on her table sat a basket full of bread. And in the mud outside... footprints. Glowing gold.
[beat 1.2 s - music swells and holds]
NARRATOR [soft, sincere, slower]: Whatever you give in love, God never forgets.
[beat 0.8 s]
NARRATOR [gentle smile in the voice]: If you needed this today, type Amen. And send it to someone who needs hope.

## Recording tips (ElevenLabs)
- Record as separate takes per character (Narrator, Maria, Stranger) with different voices; send all mp3s in order or one mixed file.
- Stability low-medium (about 35-45%) for emotion; style up a little; speed 0.9-0.95 for the narrator.
- Generate 3 takes of each key line (hook, "Come in", "Thank you, Maria", "I never told you my name", landing line) and keep the most human one.
- Leave the marked silences in the file (0.6-1.5 s). Pauses drive our scene cuts and the emotion.
- Add rain + soft strings + the final warm swell on top (or send dry and we add it in the mix).

## Animation acting notes (so it plays like a film, not a slideshow)
1. 0-3 s HOOK: macro push-in on the loaf, rain on the window, ticking; one knock hits on the word "knock". Text: ONE LOAF.
2. Maria beside sleeping Daniel; her hand rests on his hair; she rubs her tired eyes; empty bowl visible.
3. Knock x3: she freezes, head turns toward door, breath cloud; Daniel stirs.
4. Door opens slowly; wide shot: hooded stranger soaked, water dripping from hood, hand held out shaking.
5. Close-up cut: loaf -> Daniel -> loaf (eyes travel, small head turns). Hold on her swallowing.
6. She steps back and opens the door wider; stranger steps in; she closes the door behind him (real door interaction).
7. Bread tearing in her hands, two halves; she puts one half in Daniel's hand while he sleeps; offers the other to the stranger.
8. He eats slowly, a tear runs down; she watches, unsure.
9. Name line: close-up of Maria, hand over her mouth, eyes wide; camera pushes in 1 s.
10. Cut to morning light: the chair empty, the blanket folded; Maria opens the door: basket of bread on the table behind her.
11. Wide: footprints in the mud glow gold, leading away down the road (same path logic as the Stranger video); Maria and Daniel in the doorway, hand in hand.
12. Landing line on a slow pull-back; end card with CTA.
