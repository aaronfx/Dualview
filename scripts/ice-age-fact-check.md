# Fact check: "How Long Would You Last in the Ice Age?" (2026-11-13)
Method: web search (standard mode) on each claim. Several results are popular-science or news pages; where only those were found it is noted. Not a peer review.

| # | Claim in the narration | Verdict | What the sources say | Suggested wording |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "about twenty-five thousand years ago", "several degrees colder" | OK | Last Glacial Maximum is roughly 26,500-20,000 years ago; global mean cooling about 6.1 C (Tierney et al., Nature 2020) | Keep. Could say "about six degrees colder on average" |
| 2 | Bone needles "tens of thousands of years old" | OK | Denisova Cave eyed needle reported as about 50,000 years old (press); a later review lists about 40,000 cal BP | Keep "tens of thousands" |
| 3 | Homes of mammoth bones "in some places" | OK | Mezhyrich, Ukraine: about 18,000 years ago (2025 revised dating); may have been temporary shelters | Keep "in some places"; do not say 15,000 |
| 4 | Cave lions, wolves, hyenas, giant bears shared the world | OK | Cave lion, cave hyena, wolf, leopard overlapped with people in Europe; cave bears mostly ate plants | Optional: "Giant cave bears roamed too" |
| 5 | Bones and animal fat burned as fuel | MOSTLY OK | Burned bone as fuel documented at Mezhyrich, Aurignacian sites; deliberate vs accidental debated; fat evidence only suggestive | Say "People often burned bones, and likely fat" |
| 6 | Embers carried from camp to camp | NOT VERIFIED | Not searched; plausible but no source found | Say "may have carried embers" |
| 7 | "Some groups used the ice itself as a natural freezer" | WEAK | One possible meat cache on permafrost at Kammern-Grubgraben, Austria (about 23,000 years, news report of a 2021 paper) | "At least one site suggests meat was stored on frozen ground" |
| 8 | Snow goggles: "People in icy places learned to protect their eyes" | MISLEADING FOR ICE AGE | Best-supported snow goggles are Thule/Inuit walrus ivory, about 800-1200 CE; 2,000-year claims unconfirmed; none found for the Ice Age | "Much later, Arctic peoples like the Inuit carved snow goggles with narrow slits" |
| 9 | Eating snow lowers body heat; melt first | OK | Standard survival advice (energy cost of melting, hypothermia risk); mostly general outdoor sources | Keep |
| 10 | Skeletons of injured people who lived for years; "means someone looked after them" | OK, SOFTEN | Shanidar 1 (Neanderthal) with healed severe injuries; care is an interpretation some scientists dispute | "Which suggests someone looked after them" |
| 11 | Bone flutes, carved figures, painted caves | OK | Hohle Fels vulture-bone flute about 35,000-40,000 years; Venus figurine about 40,000; Chauvet cave art about 30,000-36,500 years | Keep |
| 12 | Mammoth hunting was a group job; plants scarce; wind steals heat; groups, fires and stories | GENERAL KNOWLEDGE | Not individually searched | Keep, framed as general understanding |
