## Title and metadata formula
- Pattern: `He <did a kind thing / was wronged>, Then <God rewarded him / angel appeared>`. Short, 6-9 words, a character ("He"), a moral trigger (helped / pushed / helpless) and a payoff promise (rewarded).
- Same hashtag block every time: `#JesusIsKing #Amen #jesuslovesyou #JesusIsLord`.
- Description repeats the title with emojis (praying hands, red heart) + `| #JesusIsKing`, then a 2-3 line plot summary in simple English ("A man pushed Jesus down, but instead of helping Him, people simply walked away. Nobody cared about His pain until one kind-hearted man stepped forward...").
- "Product picks" shelf with affiliate links to religious items (framed Jesus painting, cross necklaces; Meesho links). Monetisation is partly product affiliate, shown under the video.

## Visual style
- Photoreal, AI-generated live-action look (not stick figures): a rural South Asian roadside (grass, power lines, dirt/paving-stone road).
- Jesus shown realistically: white robe, red sash/cloak, crown of thorns. Miracle moments use golden light streaks, dust/debris explosions, the ground cracking open, an angel.
- Hook is visual and dramatic (ground bursting, debris flying) rather than slow storytelling.

## Story formula (from titles + description)
1. A vulnerable person (helpless man / Jesus in disguise) is wronged or ignored; bystanders walk away.
2. One ordinary kind-hearted man helps, at a cost to himself.
3. Supernatural reveal (Jesus / angel appears, golden light, ground opens).
4. Reward / blessing, shown immediately.
- This is the same moral engine as our "The Stranger" story (kindness to a stranger who is Jesus, rewarded). Strong fit for our channel.

## What to take from it for our videos
- Keep titles in the `He/She <kind act>, Then God <rewarded>` pattern; use the same hashtag block and a 2-3 line plot description with emojis.
- Open with the wrong done / ignored person within 2 seconds; make the reveal and the reward unmistakable and visual (light, glow, ground, angel).
- Output as vertical Shorts under 60 s when possible (the orientation rule still applies by length).
- Consider adding an affiliate/product shelf and a subtle on-video LIKE prompt.
