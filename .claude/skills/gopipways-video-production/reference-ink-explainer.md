## Title formula
- `What Did Ancient Humans [Actually] Do [All Day / All Night / For Pleasure / When It Rained All Week]?`; `How Did Ancient Humans [Survive / Travel] ...?`; `Why ...?`; `When Did Ancient Humans Start ...?`.
- Recurring subject token + curiosity question about everyday life + intensity words (Actually, Disturbing, TERRIFYING, Accidentally, Real Reason).

## Thumbnail formula (all 16:9)
- One recurring cartoon protagonist (brown hair, fur costume), big expressive face; 1-3 hand-lettered words with outline, almost always a question: NO SLEEP? / STILL SURVIVED? / STONE AGE PLEASURE? / NO JOBS / BORN TO KILL? / FIRST GUN? / COZY INSIDE? / TRAVEL HOW? / Why Us? / WHY AFRICA? / WHAT CHANGED? / AT AGE 12?
- Saturated colour backgrounds, small story scene behind the character, strong contrast; shock images (blood) on some. Length badge bottom-right.

## Visual/production evolution (visible in the list)
- 6 months ago: white-background doodles with simple icons and a pink/blue character (IKEA Effect, Why Clothes?, I Will Die) - low views (24k-400k).
- Last 4 months: full illustrated scenes (landscapes, caves, fire, crowds) with a recurring cast - the breakouts. Validates our move from white doodles to a continuous environment with real interactions.

## Pacing measured on their IKEA Effect video (4:09, 1280x720)
- Visual cut about every 2.8 s on average (89 cuts, median 2.6 s, longest 6.5 s; 22 scenes under 2 s, 60 scenes of 2-4 s, 8 scenes over 4 s). Elements pop in on beats (text, arrows, props), constant movement, no static shot longer than ~6 s.
- Use as our long-form target: a new picture or major change every 2-4 s, never more than 6 s.

## What to apply to OUR faith channel
1. Pick a recurring subject token like "Ancient Humans": e.g. "People in Jesus' Time" / "the Disciples" / "Bible Times". Daily-life curiosity questions: What Did Jesus Actually Do All Day? / What Did People Eat in Jesus' Time? / What Did the Disciples Do When the Storm Hit? / Where Did Jesus Sleep?
2. Series strategy: when one video wins, make 5-10 variants of the same angle.
3. Credibility: cite scripture references and sources in the description (mirrors their "all sources linked" trust signal); keep accuracy.
4. Thumbnails: our recurring characters, 1-3 word question, warm saturated colour; no gore.
5. Format split: long-form (8-12 min, landscape) for growth + Shorts (vertical, under 90 s) as funnel clips. Orientation rule still applies by length.
6. Long-form needs a reusable setting/character library (village, desert road, sea, house interior, temple, crowd) because 10 minutes at one cut per 3 s is about 200 scenes.
