04 / A few things to know

Before your
first take.

Does DualView record both cameras at once?

No. You choose the front or rear camera; the two output formats come from that single recording.

Does it record in 4K?

Auto selects the highest recording quality supported for the selected camera through Android's camera system. Available quality varies by phone and camera; 4K is not guaranteed.

Are my videos uploaded for processing?

Recording and video processing happen on your phone in the current app. Saving and sharing are actions you choose.

Does the vertical video show the entire wide scene?

It uses a crop of the source. In landscape recording, you can position the vertical crop guide before you start.

When can I download it?

DualView is in development. Launch updates will announce when it becomes available on Google Play.
