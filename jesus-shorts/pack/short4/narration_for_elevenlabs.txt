A cart broke down on a lonely road.

The stranger could not move it alone.

A rich merchant rode by on a fine horse.

He did not even slow down.

Then a poor farmer named Daniel came by, carrying his tools.

He was late for his own field, but he stopped anyway.

Together, they lifted the cart and fixed the wheel.

The stranger thanked him, and said, Your field will be blessed.

Daniel hurried to his dry, cracked ground.

Then the clouds gathered, and rain began to fall.

By harvest, his barns were the fullest in the valley.

Who is my neighbour? The one who stopped.
