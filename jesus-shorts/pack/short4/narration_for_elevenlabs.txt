She had one slice of bread, and a stranger was starving.

Mia lived with her grandmother, and they had almost nothing.

On the way home, she saw a man shaking in a doorway.

His lips were blue from the cold.

She knew her grandmother needed this bread.

But she remembered what her grandmother taught her.

She broke the bread in half, and gave him the bigger piece.

He smiled, and said, Go home, little one. Look at your door.

At home, the door was covered with bags of food.

Neighbours were cooking and laughing in their kitchen.

No one knew who had sent them.

Give, and it shall be given to you.
