Jesus came as a sick stranger, and nobody knew who he was.

A receptionist said he could not be treated without money.

A doctor walked past, and said, Not my problem.

Then a young nurse named Hannah saw him.

She had only enough money for her own mother's medicine. And she had prayed for a miracle.

But she took him in, and paid his bill with that money.

She sat beside him all night, and held his hand.

At dawn, the bed was empty, and his footprints glowed on the floor.

Her phone rang. Her mother was awake, and the sickness was gone.

She finally understood who he was.

She had welcomed her Savior.
