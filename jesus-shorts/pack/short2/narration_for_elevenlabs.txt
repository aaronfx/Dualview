A terrible storm hit the village.

At the edge of town, a beggar was shaking in the cold.

He knocked on the rich man's door.

The rich man shouted, Go away.

He knocked on three more doors. No one answered.

At the very last house, lived a poor shepherd and his small son.

The shepherd opened the door and said, We have little. But come in.

They shared their soup, and the stranger's shaking stopped.

He slept by the fire, and they gave him their only blanket.

At dawn, the stranger was gone.

Outside, their dry field had become green and full of sheep.

Some have shown kindness to angels without knowing it.
