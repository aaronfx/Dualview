Jesus came as a thirsty traveler, and nobody knew who he was.

A truck driver sped past, and splashed dust over him.

A shopkeeper said, Water is for customers.

Then a little boy named Sam saw him.

It was his last water, and he was thirsty too.

His mother had just prayed for a miracle, because their car had broken down.

But Sam walked over, and said, Here, sir, you can have it.

The stranger drank, and put a hand on his head, and blessed him.

He walked away, and his footprints began to glow.

Their car started, and a tow truck pulled up, free of charge.

They finally understood who he was.

They had welcomed their Savior.
