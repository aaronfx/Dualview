She had nine dollars, and he had nothing.

He stood in the rain, looking through the window.

Her manager said, Do not let him in.

But Grace saw how cold he was.

She slipped out the back and brought him inside.

She paid for his soup with her last nine dollars.

He ate slowly, with shaking hands.

The manager fired her on the spot.

Grace walked out into the rain with nothing.

But the stranger was gone, and a car pulled up.

The owner had watched everything. He gave her a better job that very night.

Kindness is never wasted.
