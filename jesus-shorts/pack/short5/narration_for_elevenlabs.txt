It was the coldest night of the year. One man had a coat.

A freezing man sat outside the church.

Rich people in warm clothes walked right past.

Only the old cleaner, Samuel, stayed behind.

He had one coat, and a sick daughter at home.

He looked at the freezing man, and his heart broke.

He took off his only coat, and put it around his shoulders.

He sat beside him, and shared his last bread.

Samuel walked home in the freezing night, shaking.

When he opened the door, his daughter was sitting up.

She was healed. And on the table lay his coat, warm and glowing.

When you clothe the cold, you clothe Him.
