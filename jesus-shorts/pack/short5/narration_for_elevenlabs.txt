Jesus came as a lost old man, and nobody knew who he was.

A bus driver pulled away, just as he reached the stop.

Taxi after taxi drove past, and none would stop.

Then a tired taxi driver named David slowed down.

He had one fare left, and a daughter who needed a miracle for school.

But he opened the door, and said, Get in, I will take you anywhere.

He drove all night, and refused to take a single coin.

At the end, the stranger said, God will not forget this.

His footprints began to glow.

That same morning, a scholarship letter arrived for his daughter.

He finally understood who he was.

He had welcomed his Savior.
