It was the coldest night of the year.

A shivering man sat outside the temple doors.

People in fine clothes walked right past him.

Only the old cleaner stayed behind to lock up.

Samuel had only one coat. And his daughter was sick at home.

Still, he looked at the stranger, and his heart broke.

He took off his coat, and placed it on the stranger's shoulders.

Then he shared his bread, and sat with him a while.

When he got home, he found a miracle.

His daughter was sitting up, healed, smiling.

On the table lay his coat. It was warm and glowing.

When you clothe the cold, you clothe Him.
