Everyone walked past this starving man. Except one boy.

The man had not eaten in three days.

People looked away, and some even laughed.

But Noah, just eight years old, saw him.

He had only one sandwich, and it was his lunch.

His stomach was growling too.

But he walked over, and said, Sir, please, take this.

The man cried. No one had been kind to him in years.

The man blessed him, and said, You will never go hungry.

Noah walked home with an empty bag.

But at home, his mother had a surprise.

Her prayer for work was answered that same day, and the table was full.

Whatever you do for the least of these, you do for Me.
