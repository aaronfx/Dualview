Jesus came as a freezing stranger, and nobody knew who he was.

A rich man saw him, and told him to move on.

Another woman crossed the street to avoid him.

Then a little girl named Lily stopped.

She slipped off her own scarf, and wrapped it around his neck.

Her father stood nearby, ashamed. He had prayed about rent that morning, and nothing had come.

But he sat beside the stranger, and shared his last hot soup.

The stranger stood, and thanked them, and walked slowly away.

His footprints began to glow.

That evening, the landlord called. The rent was forgiven.

They finally understood who he was.

They had welcomed their Savior.
