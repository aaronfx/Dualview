Nobody wanted to hire the stranger.

He was thin, tired, and covered in dust.

Door after door closed in his face.

Then he came to a small bakery, run by a widow named Ruth.

He asked, Please, I only need work for a day.

Ruth had barely enough bread for herself.

Still, she said, Come in. I cannot pay much, but you will not leave hungry.

All day he carried flour and fired the oven.

At sunset, she gave him half of her last loaf.

He thanked her, and walked into the night.

The next morning, her shelves were overflowing.

On the table lay a note, I was a stranger, and you took me in.

Whoever helps the least of these, helps Him.
