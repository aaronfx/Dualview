A poor boy sold fish at the market.

He needed one more sale to feed his family.

A hungry stranger approached him.

The stranger had no money. Only an empty hand.

Everyone else laughed and walked on.

The boy looked at his last fish. And thought of his own hunger.

Then he wrapped it in a cloth, and gave it away.

The stranger blessed him, and slowly disappeared into the crowd.

That evening, the boy walked home with an empty basket.

But his father was waiting at the shore, with a miracle.

Their nets were so full, they could barely pull them in.

What you give to the least, you give to Jesus.
