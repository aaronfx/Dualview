Jesus came as a poor stranger, looking for work, and nobody knew who he was.

A factory boss laughed in his face.

A store owner pointed to the door.

Then he came to a tiny bakery run by a widow named Ruth.

She had barely enough bread for herself. And she had prayed for a customer that morning.

But she said, Come in. I cannot pay you, but you will not leave hungry.

All day, he carried flour, and fed the oven, and she shared her last loaf.

At sunset, he thanked her, and walked into the night.

His footprints began to glow.

By morning, a line of customers stretched down the street, and every shelf was full.

She finally understood who he was.

She had welcomed her Savior.
