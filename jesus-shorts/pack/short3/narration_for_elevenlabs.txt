Thousands drove past. Only one man stopped.

His car had died on a cold, empty road.

Car after car sped away.

Then a tired truck driver named Daniel saw him.

Daniel was already late for his delivery.

But he pulled over anyway.

He towed the car, and gave the man his coffee.

The stranger said, God will not forget this.

Daniel got fired the next morning for being late.

But then his phone rang.

A company owner offered him a better job, with higher pay.

God sees every act of kindness.
